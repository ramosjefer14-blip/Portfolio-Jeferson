2025Project

MedSpark

Take care of your health, take care of your wallet.

A web platform that helps partner pharmacies grow and gain visibility in the market by publishing their businesses, locations, recommendations and customer comments. Plus users earn cashback, find the nearest pharmacies and buy products online from each partner pharmacy, improving the service for both pharmacies and users.

Growing economies, connecting identities.

A web platform that supports people with local businesses and cultural skills, such as making pupusas or weaving hammocks, by giving them visibility in the tourism market. They publish micro-experiences and tourists book their spots, discovering the authentic culture of El Salvador first-hand.
